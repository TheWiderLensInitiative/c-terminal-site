Why it's different

Stop watching the terminal. Let it call you.

Every phone terminal shows you a screen and leaves you staring at it. But you don't want to watch a 6-minute build, or check every few minutes whether an AI agent is stuck on a question.

C Terminal watches for you. It knows exactly when each command starts and ends, notices when a program is asking something, and connects to Claude Code and Codex. Your phone buzzes at the moment you're needed, and you answer from the notification.

How it works

Your PC runs it. Your phone keeps you posted.

Connect

The companion on your PC listens only on its Tailscale address. Your phone reaches it from anywhere, encrypted end to end by WireGuard.

Run

Start a shell, Claude Code, Codex or any command. It runs on the PC in a session that outlives the app.

Walk away

The companion watches every session: when commands end, when something asks a question, when an agent needs you.

Answer

Your phone buzzes. Allow, deny or reply right there, or open the terminal where you left it.

Install

One command on your PC. One app on your phone.

You need Tailscale (free) on both, signed in to the same account. The installer gets everything else, Python included, without sudo.

Privacy & security

Your phone, your PC, nothing in between.

C Terminal has no servers and no accounts. Read exactly what goes where →

Tailscale only

The companion listens only on your PC's Tailscale addresses, never your home network or the internet, and checks every device with tailscale whois.

Browsers can't touch it

Requests must come from the app itself, so a web page open on your phone or laptop can't drive your terminal.

Unlock to allow

Allow, Always allow and Reply on notifications need your phone unlocked. Deny never does.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is C Terminal really free?

Yes. The app and the companion are free and open source under Apache 2.0. Tailscale's free plan covers personal use.

How is this different from an SSH app?

An SSH app gives you a screen and drops your session when the phone sleeps. C Terminal keeps sessions running on your PC, needs no keys or passwords, and tells you when a command finishes, when something waits for an answer and when an AI agent needs you, so you don't have to keep checking.

Can I run Claude Code or Codex from my phone?

Yes. Tap Claude Code or Codex to start one on your PC, or run it from any shell. With cterm hooks install, Claude Code reports when it needs permission, needs you or finishes. In away mode its permission prompts come to your phone instead of the PC screen.

What happens if I don't answer an approval?

After 5 minutes the question goes back to the PC screen, exactly as if C Terminal weren't there. Turn away mode off when you're at your desk and Claude asks on the PC as usual.

Do I need to open ports on my router?

No. Tailscale connects your phone and PC directly and securely from anywhere, with no port forwarding.

Does my PC have to stay on?

Yes, your sessions live on it. The installer can keep the companion running even when nobody is logged in.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. C Terminal is Android only for now (Android 9+).
